## These alternate gene-lists do not use NDKP for gene-set curation (as some evidence of double counting due to LD in their lists due to small clumping windows). Instead, gene prioritisation based on signals from the same GWAS used for MAGMA of each adult-onset NDD. Closest gene to lead SNP used to assign gene per locus (best transferrable metric we have), with some exceptions as noted below.
